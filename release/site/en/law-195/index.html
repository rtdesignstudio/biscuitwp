Law 195/2024 in brief

Law No. 195/2024 on personal data protection has been in force since 23 August 2026 and follows the GDPR almost article by article. The list below gathers what it requires from an ordinary website: a company site, a shop, a blog with a contact form. Tick what you have done. Your ticks stay only in your browser.

1. Cookies and trackers

  • Analytics and marketing (Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, Hotjar) start only after the visitor says “Yes”. Strictly necessary cookies (session, cart, security) do not need consent.

    Law 195/2024 Art. 6–7; Law 72/2025 Art. 116(5)–(6)

    The plugin: keeps them blocked until consent.

  • The banner states the purposes briefly and links to the cookie policy, with each service, its purpose and its duration.

    Law 72/2025 Art. 116(5); Law 195/2024 Art. 12–13

    The plugin: the banner and the cookie policy, with the table built from the scan.

  • For each choice you know when it was given, for which categories and on which version of the policy.

    Law 195/2024 Art. 7(1)

    The plugin: the consent log, with CSV and JSON export.

  • A visible link on every page reopens the settings. “Reject” is as visible as “Accept”, and accepting the terms does not mean consent.

    Law 195/2024 Art. 7(3)

    The plugin: the reopen link and equal buttons, which cannot be changed.

  • Google Fonts, CDNs, Gravatar or emoji loaded from other servers receive the visitor’s IP address, which is personal data. Host them on your own server.

    Law 195/2024 Art. 5–6

    The plugin: finds them during the scan and moves Google Fonts to your server.

2. Information

  • It says who you are and how people can contact you, what data you collect and why, on what legal basis, who you share it with, how long you keep it, what rights people have and that they can complain to the National Centre for Personal Data Protection (CNPDCP).

    Law 195/2024 Art. 12–13

    The plugin: a template in Romanian, Russian and English. You: fill it in and check it.

  • Next to the contact, order or newsletter form, say briefly why you ask for the data, with a link to the policy. Ask only for what you need.

    Law 195/2024 Art. 5(1), Art. 13

    You

  • A separate checkbox, not ticked in advance, which the person can withdraw at any time, from every email.

    Law 195/2024 Art. 6–7

    You

  • Only if the site is aimed at children and relies on their consent.

    Law 195/2024 Art. 8

    You

3. People’s rights

  • An email address in the policy, for access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection.

    Law 195/2024 Art. 15–21

    You

  • The deadline can be extended by two more months for complex requests, but you tell the person, with the reason, within the first month.

    Law 195/2024 Art. 12(3)

    You

4. Providers and transfers

  • Hosting, the email service, the CRM, payments, analytics. With each one you have a contract or terms that say how they protect your data.

    Law 195/2024 Art. 28

    You

  • Data can go to countries in the European Economic Area without authorisation. For other countries, for example the US (Google, Meta), you need an adequacy decision of the CNPDCP or safeguards, for example standard contractual clauses. Mention the transfers in the policy.

    Law 195/2024 Art. 44–49

    The plugin: mentions them in the generated policy. You: check your own providers.

5. Security and incidents

  • HTTPS, software kept up to date, strong passwords and two-step authentication, backups, access to data only for those who need it.

    Law 195/2024 Art. 32

    You

  • You notify the CNPDCP within 72 hours at most of becoming aware, if the incident may put people’s rights at risk. If the risk is high, you also notify the people affected.

    Law 195/2024 Art. 33–34

    You

6. Organisation

  • For each type of data you know how long you keep it and when you delete it: form messages, orders, old accounts.

    Law 195/2024 Art. 5(1)(e)

    The plugin: the consent log deletes itself after the period you choose. You: the rest of the data.

  • It is mandatory from 250 employees. Below 250 it is also mandatory if the processing is not occasional (for example, orders or customers every day), if it may put people’s rights at risk or if it involves sensitive data.

    Law 195/2024 Art. 30

    You

  • It is mandatory for public authorities and for anyone whose core activity is monitoring people systematically and on a large scale or processing sensitive data on a large scale. It can be an employee or someone under a service contract.

    Law 195/2024 Art. 37

    You

Fines

Up to 1 000 000 MDL or 1% of turnover, and for serious infringements up to 2 000 000 MDL or 2%, whichever is higher (Art. 88). They apply gradually: 10% of the fine in the first year after entry into force, 40% in the second, 100% from the third (Art. 90(4)).

This list explains the law in plain words and does not cover every case. It is not legal advice. For your site, ask a lawyer in Moldova. The text of the law: legis.md (Romanian); in English, published by the CNPDCP: datepersonale.md. Checked on 23 November 2026.
