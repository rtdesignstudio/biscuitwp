What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website saves in your browser. This policy also covers similar technologies, for example the browser's local storage or tracking pixels.

Legal framework

Law No. 72/2025 on electronic communications, Article 116(5), allows information to be stored on or read from your device only after you have received clear and complete information about the purposes, in accordance with Law No. 195/2024 on personal data protection. For visitors from the European Union, Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) also applies.

Under Article 116(6), cookies that are strictly necessary for the website to work or for a service you request do not require consent.{{#pro}} For cookies that are not strictly necessary, we ask for your consent.{{/pro}}{{#light}} This website uses only strictly necessary cookies, so it does not ask for your consent.{{/light}}
